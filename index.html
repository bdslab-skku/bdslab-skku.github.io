Biomedical Data Science Laboratory

Bridging statistics, data science, and biomedical research.

The Biomedical Data Science Lab brings together statistics, data science, and biomedical research to address clinically meaningful questions using complex healthcare data.
We develop and apply rigorous quantitative methods across electronic health records, clinical databases, nationwide administrative data, population health surveys, and other real-world data sources.
Our work emphasizes methodological rigor, clinical relevance, and interdisciplinary collaboration to translate biomedical data into reliable and actionable evidence.

Research

Our work

We develop and apply statistical and computational methods to generate clinically meaningful evidence from complex biomedical and healthcare data.

01

Clinical Predictive Modeling

We develop statistical and machine learning approaches to predict clinically relevant outcomes and support risk assessment and decision-making in healthcare.

02

Biostatistics & Causal Inference

We develop and apply statistical methods for causal inference, comparative effectiveness research, observational studies, and clinical and trial design.

03

Real-World Data Analytics

We analyze large-scale clinical and population health data to generate real-world evidence and improve understanding of healthcare outcomes and clinical practice.

04

Healthcare AI

We apply machine learning and artificial intelligence to complex biomedical data, including structured clinical data, medical imaging, and other multimodal healthcare information.
